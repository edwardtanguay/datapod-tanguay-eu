- PROJECT: transatlantic-accent
	- characteristics
		1. non-rhoticity: drop all final Rs, not [FAH-ther] but [FAH-tha]
		2. non-verlarized R and L, i.e. speak with a light R and L instead of a dark R and L: move these two sounds from the back of your throat (Texan) to the front of the lips (British)
		3. drop alveolar tap: 
			- butter, not [BUDDER] but [BUH-tah]
			- created, not [kree-AY-ded] but [kree-AY-ted]
			- setup, not [SEH-dup] but [SETT-upp]
			- got it, not [GAWD-it]; [GOTT-itt]
			- in the city
			- water
			- the former and the latter
			- what is the matter
			- it's clear what you're saying, not [wha-chur] but [what-yoo-ah]
			- little did I know
			- it was little
		4. drop consonant syllabification
			- button, not [BUH-n] but [BUTT-en]
			- certain
			- kitten
			- mountain
			- shouldn't, not [SHU-nt] but [SHUD-ent]
		4. employ palatalization of the "u": duty is not [DOO-dee] but [DYOO-tee]
		5. drop assibilition: education is not [eh-joo-KAY-shun] but [ehd-yoo-KAY-shun]
		6. vowels are pronounced clearly, openly and deliberately: noon not [noon] but [noooon]
		7. enunciate consonants: pronounce all Ts, Bs, Ps, etc. clearly and distinctly, and non-voiced consonants crisply
		8. in general, always speak deliberately and thoughtfully, which decelerates your speech but puts you in control
		9. not British
			- "and" is flat
		- you don't have to avoid or extend contractions
			- don't and can't is ok
		- practice
			- that shouldn't matter at all
			- pour all the water in the barrel
			- it's certain that we're going to go
			- it's our duty to meet at noon on Tuesday
			- it's unopinionated
			- but that shouldn't matter
			- they haven't gotten back yet
			- out of it
			
		- drop all ums, ahs, ya knows, and likes
